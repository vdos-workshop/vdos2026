VDOS 2026 Registration

Workshop: Thursday, July 16, 2026, 2:30 PM – 6:00 PM EDT (Half-day)
Venue: Washington Hilton, Washington, D.C. (co-located with ICBO 2026 and ISMB 2026)

VDOS 2026 is a workshop of ICBO 2026, and registration is handled through the ISMB 2026 registration portal. To attend VDOS in person, register for ICBO 2026 by selecting the appropriate ICBO option during the ISMB registration process. Attending the VDOS workshop virtually does not require any registration (see Virtual Participation below).

Registration Options

ISMB + ICBO

Attend both ISMB 2026 and ICBO 2026. Registration is through the ISMB 2026 portal with an ICBO add-on fee on top of the ISMB registration fee.

ICBO Only

Attend ICBO 2026 (including VDOS) without the full ISMB conference. Registration is also through the ISMB 2026 portal.

Register via ISMB 2026 →

For the latest fees, deadlines, and one-day pass options, please see the ICBO 2026 Registration page.

Virtual Participation

Join the workshop virtually via Zoom at https://umich.zoom.us/j/94066327765 (Passcode: vdos2026).

Attending the VDOS workshop virtually only does not require any registration — it is free and open to anyone. Registration through the ISMB 2026 portal is required only if you also wish to attend the main ICBO 2026 conference (and ISMB 2026).
